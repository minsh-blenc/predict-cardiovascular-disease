Core Objective

Project Overview

The Mission

CardiacAI leverages Machine Learning to provide a non-invasive, preliminary diagnostic tool for heart disease risk assessment. Our goal is to augment clinical decision-making with data-driven intelligence.

The Dataset

The model is trained on standard, anonymized clinical datasets (similar to the Cleveland Clinic foundation). It analyzes critical metrics including blood pressure, cholesterol levels, and ECG results.

The Model

We utilize a sophisticated Classification algorithm tuned for accuracy and sensitivity. Continuous validation ensures the model provides reliable, instant risk stratification for healthcare professionals.
